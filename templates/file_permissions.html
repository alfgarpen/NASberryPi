How it works

  • Owner: The user who uploaded the file. They always have full access to their own files.
  • Visibility: Private means only the owner and admins can see/access it, even if it's in a shared folder.
  • Permissions: These flags apply to other users who have been approved for shared access.
  • Audit Logs: Denied access attempts are logged to nas_data/access_denied.log.
